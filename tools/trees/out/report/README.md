Why stock triangles stay: the contract allows a profile of k <= 3 harmonics per ring and a tolerance of 2 cm or 5 % of r. A regular n-gon is met by a round tube no better than +-r(1 - cos(pi/n))/2 (6.7 % r for hexagons), so 6-sided stock trunks thicker than about 0.45 m (the 43 m pines and spruces), the coarse sculpted trunks and limbs of the generic deciduous, dead and anomalous kinds (irregular 5-15-gon trunks of 0.5-1 m, 3-4-sided limbs; one stock triangle often spans metres, so one miss keeps all of it) and the lobed or buttressed old oaks cannot be refit inside the tolerance: their stock triangles stay, the tubes (and so the axes the new branches grow on) run inside them. Forks and crotches, flared bases and cut ends account for most of the rest. Rings whose `fit_err` is above the tolerance mark where the patches carry the shape.
